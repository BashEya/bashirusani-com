Current Research Project

View Details

Amoebae, symbionts and hydrocarbon remediation: exploring antimicrobial resistance dynamics using omics.

My doctoral research investigates free-living amoebae and their microbial symbionts in hydrocarbon-contaminated environments, their potential influence on bioremediation, and antimicrobial resistance dynamics using molecular and omics approaches.
