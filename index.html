I am a microbiologist, PhD researcher and university lecturer working at the intersection of microbial ecology, environmental microbiology, bioremediation, antimicrobial resistance, One Health and omics. My research explores how microbial interactions can help solve real-world challenges in polluted environments while supporting healthier communities and a more resilient planet.

CURRENT RESEARCH PROJECT

Amoebae, symbionts and hydrocarbon remediation: exploring antimicrobial resistance dynamics using omics.

My PhD research investigates the interactions between free-living amoebae and their microbial symbionts in hydrocarbon-contaminated environments, with a focus on bioremediation potential and antimicrobial resistance dynamics using omics approaches.
