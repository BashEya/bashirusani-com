Current Research Project

Amoebae, symbionts and hydrocarbon remediation: exploring antimicrobial resistance dynamics using omics.

My PhD research investigates the interactions between free-living amoebae and their microbial symbionts in hydrocarbon-contaminated environments, with a focus on bioremediation potential and antimicrobial resistance dynamics using omics approaches.

Research question

How do amoebae and their associated bacteria and fungi influence microbial community structure, hydrocarbon degradation and antimicrobial resistance dynamics?

Approach

Culture-based microbiology, microscopy, qPCR, 16S/ITS/18S sequencing, hydrocarbon analysis and bioinformatics-informed interpretation.

Why it matters

Understanding overlooked microbial interactions that may improve environmental remediation while informing One Health-relevant resistance ecology.
